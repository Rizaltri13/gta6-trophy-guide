    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. Story first, side strands before the endgame, then activities, collectibles, money and mayhem, and cleanup last. The platinum — Vice Got a Price! — pops when the other 54 are done.

    Missables & watch-outs

    These flags are built from the official trophy descriptions alone. Story missions themselves are not missable — they award their trophy on completion. What can close off is a side strand, a one-off challenge, or a choice you only get to make once. Where a flag is a guess rather than a certainty, the guide says so plainly.

    Next up

    What to chase next, picked from the roadmap: missables and open strands first, then the stage you are furthest through.
